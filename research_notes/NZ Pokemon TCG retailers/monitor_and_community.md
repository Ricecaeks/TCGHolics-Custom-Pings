# Zephyr monitor coverage & community-sourced NZ Pokémon TCG store list

Research date: 2026-11-04. Scope: the publicly advertised coverage of a restock monitor called "Zephyr", plus community-named NZ online stores for Pokémon TCG (preorder / release-day focus), their platforms, and 30th Celebration / Mega Evolution availability. Public info only — no private/gated Discord channels accessed.

## Is there a NZ/AU Pokémon or TCG restock-monitor called "Zephyr"? What does it publicly say it monitors?

### Takeaway
No public evidence was found for a Pokémon/TCG restock monitor named "Zephyr" serving NZ or AU. Across six varied searches (direct name, + "discord", + "sneaker/pokemon", + "twitter", + "australia new zealand", and an extended search) nothing surfaced a website, Discord landing page, or X/Twitter account for such a service. Treat its existence and any advertised store list as **unverified / not found in public sources**.

### Cited Findings
- A direct search for a "Zephyr monitor discord Pokemon restock Australia New Zealand" returned only generic restock Discords and the Pokémon Center AU/NZ launch — no Zephyr match — [WebSearch result summary](https://discord.com/servers/pokemon-restocks-and-alerts-1369077918244012072).
- "Zephyr" as a term mostly resolves to unrelated brands (Zephyr Headwear, the Zephyr Project) rather than any restock tool — [Zephyr Headwear, Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zephyr_Headwear).
- The only "zephyr" hit adjacent to TCG was a TCGSync store product/collection URL (`admintcg.tcgsync.com/products/zephyr`), which appears to be a product listing on the TCGSync storefront platform, not a monitoring service — [tcgsync.com product path](https://admintcg.tcgsync.com/products/zephyr).
- An extended search specifically for `"zephyr" pokemon restock discord server alerts stores` again returned only unrelated servers (PokePings, Pokemon Restocks and Alerts, Poke RestockR, PokeNotify, PokeChap) with no Zephyr entry — [PokeNotify Discord page](https://www.pokenotify.com/discord).

### Inferences
- If "Zephyr" exists, it is likely a small/private Discord-first monitor not indexed on Disboard/top.gg/discord.com discovery, or it is branded differently on its public pages. The caller should supply an invite/handle for a targeted lookup; from public indexes it is effectively invisible as of late 2026.
- The TCGSync "zephyr" URL is almost certainly a coincidental product name on a store that uses TCGSync, not the monitor in question.

### Gaps
- Could not confirm or deny a "Zephyr" monitor. No website, Discord invite, or social handle located. Advertised store list / logos therefore could not be captured.

## Regional restock Discords/monitors that do or could cover NZ (context for the long tail)

### Takeaway
No NZ-only Pokémon restock Discord was found in public directories; the nearest regional servers are UK- and Canada-specific. Most large servers are US-centric. This matters because it means NZ collectors largely rely on store-native stock, Trade Me, and Facebook groups rather than a dominant NZ monitor.

### Cited Findings
- **Celadon** — covers "deals, new releases, pre-orders, and restock notifications" but for **Canada** — [discord.com/servers/celadon](https://discord.com/servers/celadon-826259609122897941).
- **PokéMart UK** and **PokemonStockUK** — UK-only restock servers — [PokéMart UK](https://discord.com/servers/pokemart-uk-restock-alerts-1350554582258028644); [PokemonStockUK](https://discord.com/servers/pokemonstockuk-1403310541174341813).
- **PackPing** — "instant TCG drop & restock alerts … across 50+ stores in Canada & the US" (North America only) — [PackPing](https://discord.com/servers/packping-1524905895467679875).
- **PokeNotify** promotional listing claims it is the pick for collectors outside the US, covering "Canada, UK and Australia" (promotional; AU mention but not NZ-specific; treat with caution) — [tcgdropradar.com](https://tcgdropradar.com/).
- Large general servers: **Pokemon Restocks & News** (~82,051 members), **Restockd / Pokemon Restocks**, **Pokemon Restocks and Alerts** ("over 100 retailers … 100% free") — region-agnostic, US-weighted — [Pokemon Restocks & News](https://discord.com/servers/pokemon-restocks-and-news-1182136115981996033); [Pokemon Restocks and Alerts](https://discord.com/servers/pokemon-restocks-and-alerts-1369077918244012072).

### Inferences
- The absence of an NZ-dedicated monitor is the market gap TCGHolics is targeting; the long tail of NZ stores is not comprehensively covered by any public monitor found.

### Gaps
- Could not verify PokeNotify's AU/NZ store coverage from a primary source (claim is on a promotional aggregator).

## What NZ online stores do collectors name as release-day / preorder sources?

### Takeaway
A consistent set of NZ online stores recurs for Pokémon TCG and preorders: **Otakumart, Fire Spin Hobby, the Pokémon Center NZ store, Mighty Ape**, plus the Shopify shops TCGHolics already tracks (Card Merchant NZ, Iron Knight Gaming). Community discussion also points to **Trade Me, Facebook Marketplace/NZ Pokémon groups, and the FetchTCG app** for singles/secondary sourcing. The big-box channels (Warehouse, Farmers, Kmart, JB Hi-Fi, Noel Leeming, Toyworld) are covered in the repo's RETAILERS.md and are the main release-day drops; the items below are the long-tail specialist stores.

### Cited Findings
- **Otakumart** — `otakumart.co.nz`. Sells Pokémon TCG, Yu-Gi-Oh! and Magic; ships from a South Auckland warehouse; runs in-store events and buys cards. Many product pages are explicitly marked preorder (e.g. "League Battle Deck: Charizard ex … pre-order item and will ship on release") — [Otakumart home](https://otakumart.co.nz/); [League Battle Deck listing](https://otakumart.co.nz/products/league-battle-deck-charizard-ex-pokemon-tcg).
- **Fire Spin Hobby** — self-describes as "New Zealand's premier destination for authentic Pokemon Trading Card Game products"; offers a refund if authenticity can't be verified (seller's own claims). Public site seen at a temporary host `firespin.170.64.171.111.sslip.io` (an sslip.io IP-based dev/preview URL — suggests a self-hosted or in-migration storefront) — [Fire Spin Hobby](https://firespin.170.64.171.111.sslip.io/).
- **Pokémon Center NZ** — official store `pokemoncenter.com/en-nz`, launched for AU/NZ in 2024 after US/CA/UK; ships only within NZ; region-locked to visitors outside supported regions — [Pokémon.com announcement](https://www.pokemon.com/us/pokemon-news/pokemon-center-now-available-in-australia-and-new-zealand); [Inside Retail NZ](https://insideretail.co.nz/2024/06/19/pokemon-launches-online-stores-for-australia-new-zealand/).
- **Mighty Ape** (`mightyape.co.nz`) — named as a major NZ retailer; repo notes it is online-only and behind DataDome. Already listed in RETAILERS.md (recon pending) — [RETAILERS.md, repo].
- **Card Merchant NZ** (`cardmerchant.co.nz`) and **Iron Knight Gaming** (`ironknightgaming.co.nz`) — both confirmed **Shopify** and verified live by TCGHolics on 2026-10-29; Card Merchant is a large singles shop (~9,750 products Oct 2026) — [RETAILERS.md, repo, "Shopify (generic)" section].
- Community sourcing (NZ Reddit thread, via third-party mirror — verify on original): collectors name **Trade Me**, **Facebook Marketplace** via "trusted sellers in NZ Pokémon groups", trade days at local stores, and the **FetchTCG app** ("has many NZ sellers") — [Reddit snapshot mirror](https://reddit.sentinel-team.org/posts/1unycj0/snapshots/2026-07-07T06%3A29%3A10.35744Z).
- Bulbapedia (possibly dated) states the TCG is "difficult to find in New Zealand stores, but can still be purchased online" — [Bulbapedia: Pokémon in New Zealand](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Tv2).

### Inferences
- Otakumart is the single most-cited NZ specialist for both in-stock and preorder TCG; it should be a priority long-tail target.
- Fire Spin Hobby's IP/sslip.io URL implies a non-Shopify, self-hosted stack (platform unconfirmed) — worth a direct recon for monitorability.
- The toy/hobby retailers Toyco, Shining Gems, Grey Ogre Games, 123 Cards, Whatnot/Collectables could not be confirmed from public search in the time available and remain candidates, not confirmed stores.

### Gaps
- Could not retrieve r/PokemonTCGNZ or r/newzealand threads directly (searches returned no live subreddit threads). NZ Facebook group store lists were not publicly indexed.
- Toyco, Shining Gems, Grey Ogre Games, 123 Cards unverified — no supporting public page found.

### Gaps

- Fire Spin Hobby platform unknown and its permanent domain unconfirmed (only an IP/sslip.io preview host was indexed).

## Which stores carry the 30th Celebration / Mega Evolution range (preorder or in-stock)?

### Takeaway
Otakumart and Face to Face Games both list the full Mega Evolution 30th Celebration range (ETB, Knock Out Collection, mini tins, booster bundle, figure collections). Otakumart notably switched some 30th items to **in-store-only, no preorder** due to limited allocation — a signal that release-day in-store monitoring matters for this range in NZ.

### Cited Findings
- **Otakumart** — 30th Celebration Knock Out Collection listed at NZ$24, fulfilment on/after 2026-09-16, but **"due to anticipated limited allocations, we will NOT be accepting pre-orders. Stock will be available IN-STORE ONLY at release, while supplies last."** The 30th Elite Trainer Box page is marked "Release Status: Pre-order", initial release September 2026; Mew Figure Collection listed for November 2026 — [Knock Out Collection](https://otakumart.co.nz/products/30th-celebration-knock-out-collection-pokemon-tcg); [30th ETB](https://otakumart.co.nz/products/30th-celebration-elite-trainer-box-pokemon-tcg); [Mew Figure Collection](https://otakumart.co.nz/products/30th-celebration-figure-collection-mew-pokemon-tcg).
- **Face to Face Games** — 30th Celebration ETB listed at 249.99 with "PRE-ORDER NOW FOR SHIPPING ON 2026-09-16"; Mini Tin (Night Pikachu) release 2026-10-02; also Day Pikachu mini tin and booster bundle (currency/NZ shipping not stated on page) — [30th ETB](https://facetofacegames.com/products/pokemon-tcg-mega-evolution-30th-celebration-elite-trainer-box); [Night Pikachu mini tin](https://facetofacegames.com/products/pokemon-tcg-mega-evolution-30th-celebration-mini-tin-night-pikachu).
- Otakumart preorder policy generally: "if we cannot fulfill a pre-order for any reason, you receive a full refund"; "release dates are estimates only" — [Otakumart preorder listing](https://otakumart.co.nz/products/greninja-ex-special-collection-pokemon-tcg).

### Inferences
- For the 30th / Mega Evolution range in NZ, in-store-only allocation at specialist stores (Otakumart) reinforces the value of the repo's nationwide in-store alerts (Warehouse `FIND_IN_STORE`, Farmers/Kmart per-store stock).
- Face to Face Games appears to be an AU-facing Shopify store (`/en-intl/`); confirm it ships to NZ before treating it as an NZ release-day source.

### Gaps
- NZ-shipping confirmation for Face to Face Games and Cardbot not established.
- Fire Spin Hobby, Card Merchant, Iron Knight, Mighty Ape 30th-range listings not individually checked this session.
