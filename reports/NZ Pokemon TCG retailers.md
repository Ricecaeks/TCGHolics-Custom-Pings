# Twenty-three Zephyr stores expand TCGHolics' NZ coverage

Zephyr Monitors pinged **47 distinct New Zealand stores** during the owner's trial (about 14 October to 3 November 2026). **27 of them sell card games**, and **23 of those 27 are not yet monitored** by the TCGHolics bot ([Zephyr alert history](../research_notes/NZ%20Pokemon%20TCG%20retailers/zephyr_alert_history.md)). Most of the 23 came through a Zephyr feed labelled "Shopify". The bot already has a generic Shopify adapter, so about 20 of them can probably go in with the existing `/retailer add-shopify` command once each store's platform is checked. Only Otakumart and Hobby Zone have been confirmed as Shopify so far. The stores that matter most right now are the ones listing **ME06 Delta Reign**, which releases on **6 November 2026**: Game Corner, OCARE, Aniverse, PLAYX, The Game Tree, Collectors Guild, Kidzstuffonline, Card Masters, Game Roost and Toy Time. Several of them already show per-customer limits of 1 or 2. For the **Mega Evolution: 30th Celebration** range, the strongest new targets are Otakumart, Game Corner, OCARE, PLAYX, MetaLife and Kidzstuffonline. Some non-Shopify stores are worth a new adapter: Paper Plus (151 card-game alerts), Mighty Ape, Fire Spin Hobby (WooCommerce), Games Lab (Lightspeed) and the official Pokémon Center NZ. Everything else in the research is closed, Australian, not a card shop, or spam, and should be left out.

## Delta Reign lands 6 November with tight limits at ten stores

**ME06 Delta Reign releases on 6 November 2026**, two days after this report. The Game Tree lists that release date next to "strict limits" on its booster packs and bundles. Collectors Guild caps its Delta Reign booster bundle and booster box bundles at **1 per customer** ([Zephyr alert history](../research_notes/NZ%20Pokemon%20TCG%20retailers/zephyr_alert_history.md)). Ten new targets showed Delta Reign product during the trial: Game Corner, OCARE, Aniverse, PLAYX, Card Masters, Kidzstuffonline, The Game Tree, Game Roost, Collectors Guild and Toy Time. All ten are Group B stores, so they can likely be added in minutes. **Adding them before 6 November is the most useful single action from this research.** Iron Knight's only Delta Reign listing is a nine-pocket binder, not cards ([specialty store notes](../research_notes/NZ%20Pokemon%20TCG%20retailers/specialty_card_stores.md)).

The **30th Celebration** set has been on sale since **16 September 2026**. Some later collections were also dated 6 November, and EB Games Australia listed the 30th Ultra-Premium Collection (Espeon) for 6 November with a limit of one per customer ([EB Games AU](https://www.ebgames.com.au/product/other/378902-pokemon-tcg-30th-celebration-ultra-premium-collection-espeon); [Bulbagarden](https://bulbagarden.net/threads/new-cards-and-product-lineup-revealed-for-the-pokemon-trading-card-games-upcoming-30th-celebration-expansion.310971/)). In NZ, **Otakumart has the deepest range**. It carries 192 30th Celebration singles and sealed preorders, including the Ultra-Premium Collection Day at NZ$435 and the Elite Trainer Box ([Otakumart UPC Day](https://otakumart.co.nz/products/30th-celebration-ultra-premium-collection-day-pokemon-tcg); [Otakumart ETB](https://otakumart.co.nz/products/30th-celebration-elite-trainer-box-pokemon-tcg)). Otakumart also refused preorders on the 30th Knock Out Collection, saying stock would be sold **"IN-STORE ONLY at release"** because allocations were limited ([Otakumart Knock Out Collection](https://otakumart.co.nz/products/30th-celebration-knock-out-collection-pokemon-tcg)). Game Corner and OCARE both listed the Ultra-Premium Collection Day/Night. PLAYX listed the Knock Out Collection with a limit of 2. MetaLife listed the 30th Battle Deck and Kidzstuffonline the 30th Booster Bundle. On the big-box side, Farmers and The Warehouse carried 30th bundles and tins ([Zephyr alert history](../research_notes/NZ%20Pokemon%20TCG%20retailers/zephyr_alert_history.md); [RETAILERS.md](../docs/RETAILERS.md)).

Limits are now common: 1 at Collectors Guild, 2 at PLAYX, 3 at Card Merchant and 10 at The Warehouse. Retailers are rationing allocations, so restocks will be brief. A bot that only reports "in stock" will lag a bot that also reports **new preorder listings** and **limit changes**. Many big-box Pokémon listings are in-store only (Kmart `FulfilmentChannel: 2`, The Warehouse `FIND_IN_STORE`), so the bot's existing in-store alerts are still the main signal for chains ([RETAILERS.md](../docs/RETAILERS.md)).

## Shopify feeds are easy to monitor; the other platforms each need their own adapter

For Group A and B stores, the generic adapter polls `/products.json`, which lists the most recently updated products first, and reads each variant's `available` flag. Card Merchant's `pokemon-sealed` and `pre-orders` collections show what to look for: collection handles such as `pokemon-sealed`, `preorders` or `30th-celebration-singles-pokemon-tcg` work as optional narrower feeds ([specialty store notes](../research_notes/NZ%20Pokemon%20TCG%20retailers/specialty_card_stores.md)). Stores write the set name inconsistently ("Mega Evolution: 30th Celebration", "ME06 Delta Reign", "30th Ultra Premium"). The keyword filter should therefore match **30th Celebration, Mega Evolution, Delta Reign, ME06, Ascended Heroes, Perfect Order, Phantasmal Flames, Pitch Black, Chaos Rising and Ultra-Premium Collection**. It should exclude "Battle Figure" so Animal Kingdoms' toys don't trigger alerts. The same stores also gave One Piece (OP-18, EB-05, TS-03), Magic, Lorcana, Riftbound and Yu-Gi-Oh alerts, so a per-game filter would serve other TCG channels without adding stores.

The non-Shopify group has four platform families. **WooCommerce** (Fire Spin Hobby) has a public product API, so one adapter would cover it and any later WooCommerce card shop. **Lightspeed** (Games Lab) has no JSON feed, but its category page `/products/pokemon` is stable enough to parse ([hobby shop notes](../research_notes/NZ%20Pokemon%20TCG%20retailers/hobby_game_shops.md)). **ComicHub** hosts NZ comic shops at `stores.comichub.com/<shop>`, so one adapter could cover Heroes for Sale and probably Comics Compulsion. **Custom enterprise sites** (Paper Plus, Mighty Ape, Pokémon Center NZ, Premium Bandai) each need their own recon. Paper Plus is the best of these because of its alert volume. Mighty Ape and the Pokémon Center are high value but bot-protected or region-locked, so they should only be added if their public pages can be monitored as they are. Fire Spin was first indexed on a temporary `sslip.io` IP host. Use the canonical `firespin.co.nz` instead.

## Conclusion

Most of NZ's specialist card shops run Shopify, so the bot can cover them with the adapter it already has. They are not hidden behind custom platforms. The main gap was not knowing which stores exist, and Zephyr's trial has largely answered that. The remaining platform work is short: about 20 quick JSON checks, then four small adapters (WooCommerce, Lightspeed, ComicHub and Paper Plus), ordered by how much card-game traffic each store showed.

Store size is not a good guide to priority. Small Shopify shops such as Collectors Guild and The Game Tree set the tightest limits and listed Delta Reign earliest. Otakumart is keeping some 30th stock for in-store sale. For short drops with purchase limits, the most useful alerts are new preorder listings at independent shops, plus the per-store signals the bot already gets from the chains. A large catalogue matters less than early warning.
